US National Debt

Below are some visualizations redarging the US National Debt from January 1, 2016 to January 1, 2026. This time frame was chosen for ease of viewing and simplicity. Total Debt is broken into two categories, Intragovernmental Holdings and Debt Held by the Public. Intragovernmental Holdings in in regards to the government owing itself money. Things such as trust funds for programs, borrowing for certain spikes in spending, and a small amount of marketable securities held by the government fall into this category. The Debt Held by the Public includes all other debt held by private citizens or organizations. This inculdes domestic and foreign investments in bonds and such.

General Visualization

This visualization is very basic and conveys to readers that the debt is steadily increasing. It also allows for the 2 categories of debt to be compared with ease. This visualization is great for basic ideas regarding the national debt.


Political Visualization

This visualization has a political implication. The times highlighted in red are when Donald Trump was the president. This allows readers to contextualize this data with the presidential leadership.


Stacked Visualization

This visualization shows the two types of debt stacked on top of each other. The total debt is still shown through the addition of these density plots. This visualization, while a bit more difficult to see the values of Debt Held by the Public, can be useful in showing just how much bigger the public debt is than the government debt.


Percentage Visualization

This visualization, especially when paired with the previous one, shows information about the share of each type of debt. It clearly shows the share of the total debt that each type has. It also has a very unusual look to it that gets the readers mind thinking.
