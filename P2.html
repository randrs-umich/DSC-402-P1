DWI Costs Visualization

Below are 2 visualizations. The first visualization was taken from vis.wtf and depicts the costs associated with getting a DWI (Driving While Intoxicated) charge in Travis County, Texas. The second visualization is of the same data just much easier to read and understand.

Original Visualization

This visualization has several glaring problems. The use of the third dimention is not useful in this visualization and actively makes it more difficult to understand. Had the third dimention not been utilized, this visualization would read a lot better. The texture used to color the bars is extremely difficult to understand. The bars are 3D but the texture and colors almost make it look like the bars are 2d objects due to no edges being visible and the colors of the sides and tops of the bars being similar. Most of the visualization just looks like 1 big blob of texture. These issues make this visualization extremely difficult to read and understand.


Improved Visualization

This visualization is simple and much easier to read than the original. It is a 2 dimentional vertical bar chart with a solid color filling the bars. The labels at the bottom are easily discernible from each other. This simplicity makes this chart extremely easy to read and draw conclusions from.
